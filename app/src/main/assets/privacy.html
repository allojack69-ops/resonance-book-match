Privacy Policy

Резонанс / Book Match

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Data stored by the app

The app stores a randomly generated profile identifier, reading-preference signals, recommendation feedback, and the preference graph locally on the user's Android device. This information is used only to personalize recommendations inside the app.

Data transmission

The app does not send the profile, reading preferences, recommendation feedback, or exported JSON to the developer or to third parties. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or account system.

Export

The user may explicitly export their profile as a JSON file to the device's Downloads folder. The app does not upload that file.

Deletion

The locally stored profile can be deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Exported JSON files remain under the user's control and can be deleted from the device's file manager.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact information provided on the app's Google Play listing.
